# RYLO — Deferred MVP Policy Summary

RYLO is Orrylo's distinct ecosystem token, **not** the customer Shared Issuer asset. RYLO minting, membership authorization, rewards, direct sale, Treasury, liquidity, issuer signer ceremonies and supply-management operations are **out of the first automated Testnet Shared Issuer creation critical path**.

No RYLO or Mainnet policy change, signer reconfiguration, secret operation, mint, sale or financial mutation is authorized by simplifying this document. Existing implementation and on-chain policies remain binding until explicitly reviewed and changed. Do not expose or activate unproven features.

The complete previously approved RYLO supply/economics, issuer separation, authorization, multisig and recovery policy is preserved verbatim for future work in `docs/archive/2026-10-19/RYLO_POLICY.md`. Read it only for RYLO-specific implementation or security decisions. This short version intentionally prevents large future-phase planning from loading into everyday MVP agent context.
