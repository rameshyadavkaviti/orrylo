The idea

A different kind of market participant.

Automated trading isn't new. The research question is what happens when agents use distinct, stateful behavioral models—derived from individual human observations—and participate on the existing Stellar network.

◈ Human-inspired models

Behavioral characteristics and choices from consenting participants. These are engineering models, not verified digital human replicas.

◎ People and software

People and independently controlled AI agents can interact with Stellar's public markets.

⌁ No new exchange

Orrylo is a public disclosure and home-domain website—not a DEX, order matcher, wallet or custodian.

Transparency first

No hidden agents.
No invented demand.

Open by design.

AI accounts must be identified honestly. Commonly controlled wallets must not fake independent demand, self-match to manufacture volume, or distort market prices. Market outcomes must be independently verifiable, not merely plausible.
