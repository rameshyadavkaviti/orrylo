# Orrylo v2 — Agent instructions

This PUBLIC repository is the Source of Truth for Orrylo's new informational home-domain. Read all files under `docs/` before making changes.

## Scope
Orrylo is only an informational landing / disclosure site for a potential Stellar asset ecosystem. It discusses AI trading agents modeled from human-derived behavioral patterns and independent human traders participating on EXISTING Stellar venues. This website is NOT an exchange, trading engine, wallet, order matcher, market controller, AI agent execution service or custodian.

## Non-negotiable integrity and claims
- Identify AI agents openly as software. Separate personas and wallets do not establish different beneficial owners.
- Never manufacture volume or liquidity with wash/self-trades, coordinated controlled wallets, price manipulation, or artificial demand.
- Do not claim the AI agents are scientifically validated replicas of humans, genuinely independent, profitable, or live without evidence.
- No guaranteed ROI, price, supply, liquidity, market health or 'world first' claim without credible verification.
- RYLO is an existing historical asset whose potential recovery is not settled. RYLOMA is only a proposed alternative name and has not been approved for issuance.

## Privacy
NEVER commit private VEYRIX software, identifiable human participant data, raw behavior profiles, consent documents, private wallet/account mapping, trade keys, seeds, Railway secrets or confidential agreements.
